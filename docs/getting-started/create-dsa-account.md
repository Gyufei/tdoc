# Create DSA Account

A [DeFi Smart Account (DSA)](/docs/overview/what-s-dsa.md) is a personal account on the [Monad network](https://www.monad.xyz/) that allows you to manage your assets, trade tokens, and interact with DeFi protocols securely.  
This guide helps you quickly create your [DSA account](/docs/overview/what-s-dsa.md) on [Tadle](https://v3.tadle.com/) using the [Monad network](https://www.monad.xyz/).

---

## Quick Guide:

### 1. Open Tadle
- Go to [Tadle v3](https://v3.tadle.com).
- Make sure your wallet is connected to the **Monad Network**.


### 2. Connect Wallet
- Connect your wallet on the **Monad Network**.
- Ensure you have enough **$MON** to cover gas fees.

### 3. Create DSA Account
- Click **"Create DSA Account"**.

### 4. Confirm Transaction
- Confirm the transaction in your wallet to finalize the account creation.

### 5. Verify Account
- Return, your DSA account will now be visible and ready to use.
- Click on your newly created [DSA account](/docs/overview/what-s-dsa.md) to view the address and more details.

## Notes
- You can create **multiple DSA accounts** with a single wallet.  
- Each DSA account can assign **Authorities** to **multiple wallets**, allowing shared management.  
- After creation, your [DSA account](/docs/overview/what-s-dsa.md) can be used for trading, market creation, and other Monad activities.
