# Create DSA account

Follow these steps to quickly create your DSA account on Tadle:



1. Go to [Tadle v3](https://v3.tadle.com).
2. Connect your wallet on **Monad**.  
3. Click **"Create DSA Account"** 
4. Confirm the transaction in your wallet.  




> **Note:**  
> - Always confirm you are on the **Monad** network.  
> - Ensure you have enough native tokens (**MON**) to cover gas fees.
