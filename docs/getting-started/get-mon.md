# Get $MON


Tadle's [Token Station](https://v3.tadle.com/token-station) offers two ways to **Top up $MON**  :

- **Ethereum via CCIP**
- **Base Network via Bridge Router**

Select **Ethereum** or **Base** to complete your exchange quickly and securely.

---


## Quick Guide: 


### 1. Switch Network
- Switch your wallet to **Ethereum** or **Base**.
- The [Token Station](https://v3.tadle.com/token-station) will open automatically after switching.

### 2. Choose Token
- Select the token you want to swap: **ETH, USDT, or USDC**.

### 3. Select Target Asset
- Choose **MON** as the token to receive(or [monUSD](/docs/core-modules/mon-usd.md) as an alternative)..

### 4. Optional: Specify Recipient
- (Optional) Enter another wallet address if you want the tokens sent to a different address.

### 5. Top Up
- Ensure your wallet is on the **correct network** (**Ethereum** or **Base**) before proceeding.
- Ensure you have enough native tokens (**ETH** or **Base ETH**) to cover network/gas fees.
- Click **Top Up** to complete the swap and receive your tokens.


## Notes
- It is **not recommended** to swap amounts smaller than **$2**, as the ftransaction fees are relatively high.
- After completing the swap, you can view your balance on [Monad Vision](https://testnet.monadexplorer.com/).
- Please note that transfers may take some time to complete - please allow a few minutes for confirmation.
