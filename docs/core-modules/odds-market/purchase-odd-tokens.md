# Purchase Odd Tokens


Tadle provides two modes for purchasing **Odd Tokens**:


1. **Fast Mode**  
    - Executes your purchase immediately at the current market price.  
    - Ideal for users who want to quickly acquire tokens without specifying a price.  

2. **Limit Mode**  
   - Allows you to set a specific price for your purchase.  
   - The order will only execute when the market reaches your specified price.  
   - Useful for users who want more control over the execution price.

---

## Quick Guide


### 1. Open a Market
- Go to the **[Tadle Odds Markets](https://v3.tadle.com/odds/markets)** page.
- Select and open the market you want to trade in.

### 2. Choose Purchase Mode
- Select your desired **purchase mode**:
  - **Fast Mode** for quick execution.
  - **Limit Mode** for precise control of your purchase price.

### 3. Select the Token
- Choose the **Odd Token** you want to buy.

### 4. (For Limit Mode) Set Target Price
- If using Limit Mode, enter your **target price** for the purchase.

### 5. Enter Purchase Amount
- Specify the **amount** of tokens you wish to buy.

### 6. Confirm the Purchase
- Review your order and confirm to execute the trade.

## Tips
- Ensure your wallet is connected to **Monad** and your **[DSA account](/docs/overview/what-s-dsa.md)** is ready.
- **Fast Mode** ensures quick execution but may result in a different price than expected.
- **Limit Mode** may take longer to execute but allows precise control over your purchase price.
- If no shares are available, your order will be placed in the **order book**.
- Orders can be cancelled anytime via **[Dashboard](https://v3.tadle.com/odds/dashboard/trade) → [Trade](https://v3.tadle.com/odds/dashboard/trade) → Open Orders**.
