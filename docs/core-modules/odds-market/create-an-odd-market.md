# Create an odd market

The **Odds Market** on Tadle is a decentralized prediction and trading platform where users can **create, trade, and resolve markets** based on real-world events or conditions. It allows participants to speculate on outcomes and earn rewards based on accurate predictions.

Follow these steps to create and manage a market on Tadle Odds:


---

## Quick Guide
1. Go to [Tadle Odds](https://v3.tadle.com/odds/) 
2. Connect your wallet on **Monad Network**.  
3. Open **Dashboard → Portfolio → Create Market**.  
4. Complete all fields and click **"Create Market"**.  
5. Confirm the transaction in your wallet.  
6. Monitor your market in **Dashboard** and **Portfolio**.



> **Tips:**  
> - Double-check all fields before creating a market.  
> - Make sure your wallet is connected to the **Monad Network** and your DSA account is ready.
> - After creating a market, monitor its status via **Dashboard** and **Portfolio**.  
> - Keep resolver contract information accurate to ensure correct market resolution.
