# Create an Odd Market

The [**Odds Market**](https://v3.tadle.com/odds/markets) on [Tadle](https://v3.tadle.com/) is a decentralized prediction and trading platform where users can **create, trade, and resolve markets** based on real-world events or conditions. It allows participants to speculate on outcomes and earn rewards based on accurate predictions.

Follow these steps to create and manage a market on [Tadle Odds](https://v3.tadle.com/odds/markets):


---

## Quick Guide: 


### 1. Open Tadle Odds
- Go to [Tadle Odds](https://v3.tadle.com/odds/markets).

### 2. Connect Your Wallet
- Connect your wallet on the **Monad Network**.
- Ensure your **[DSA account](/docs/overview/what-s-dsa.md)** is ready.

### 3. Navigate to Market Creation
- Open **[Dashboard](https://v3.tadle.com/odds/dashboard/) → [Portfolio](https://v3.tadle.com/odds/dashboard/portfolio) → Create Market**.

### 4. Fill in Market Details
- Complete all required fields for your market.
- Click **"Create Market"** to proceed.

### 5. Monitor Your Market
- Track your newly created market via **[Dashboard](https://v3.tadle.com/odds/dashboard/) → [Trade](https://v3.tadle.com/odds/dashboard/trade)**.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before creating a market to avoid errors.
- Newly created markets are set to **offline** by default.
